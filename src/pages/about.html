The name on the van answers the phone

Scott Lind Electric is a state-licensed electrical contractor based on Taylor Avenue in Idaho Falls, working across residential, commercial and industrial jobs throughout eastern Idaho.

The whole company is built on one decision: take the small jobs. Most electricians won't drive across town for a single dead outlet — there is a subdivision to wire and the math doesn't work. We took the other view. Fix the small thing well, and when something serious happens in that house or that building, there is no question who gets the call.

That is also why there is no service-call fee inside 30 miles, why estimates are free, and why the phone gets answered after hours. Not marketing — just the only way the promise holds up.
