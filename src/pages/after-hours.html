Do this right now

  1. Smell burning or see smoke?
    Shut off the breaker feeding that area if you can reach it safely. If there is fire or smoke in the wall, call 911 first.
  2. Water near electrical?
    Don't touch the panel or any wet device. Kill power at the main if you can do it from a dry position, then call.
  3. Breaker won't reset?
    Try it once. If it trips straight back, leave it off — it is holding back a fault. Don't tape or wedge it.
  4. Then call {{site.phone}}
    Tell us what you saw, smelled and heard. That detail is usually enough for us to arrive with the right parts.

After-hours questions

Do you answer the phone at night?

We answer after-hours calls and will tell you honestly whether we can be there tonight or first thing in the morning. If it's dangerous, we treat it as dangerous.

What counts as an electrical emergency?

Anything hot, smoking, sparking or wet, a breaker that will not hold, a total loss of power to the building, or a fault that stops a business trading. The table above covers the common cases.

Which areas do you cover after hours?

Idaho Falls, Ammon and the surrounding communities within 30 miles — Ucon, Iona, Shelley, Rigby, Ririe and Roberts included.

Should I shut off the main breaker?

If you can reach it safely and dryly and something is smoking, sparking or wet — yes, then call. If reaching it means standing in water or touching a hot panel, stay back and call from a safe distance.
