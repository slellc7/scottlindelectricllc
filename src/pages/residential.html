Repair it or replace it?

The honest answer depends on what failed. Here is how we decide — the same reasoning we'll walk you through on site, before you spend anything.

When we repair a component and when we replace it
ComponentRepair makes senseReplace makes sense
Outlet / switchLoose connection, tripped GFCIScorching, melted face, cracked body
BreakerTrips only under real overloadTrips with no load, feels hot, won't reset
PanelSpace available, brand still supportedFull, obsolete, corroded, or known-defective
FixtureBallast or socket onlyFailing housing, or converting to LED
WiringSingle damaged runWidespread damage or unsafe original work
